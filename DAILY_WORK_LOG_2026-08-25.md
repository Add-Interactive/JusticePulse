## 1. Executive Summary

On **August 25, 2026**, Add Interactive Studios engineered and deployed a dedicated, all-in-one **ICE & Specialized Tactical Police Squad Encounter Defense & Rights Shield System** for **Justice Pulse** on behalf of **NEXT Media**:

1. **Digital Red Card (Tarjeta Roja) Modal**: Full-screen high-contrast constitutional display to press against closed windows or doors with multi-lingual audio text-to-speech speaker broadcast.
2. **10-Language Audio Announcer**: Web Speech neural synthesis speaking 5th Amendment silence and 4th Amendment search refusal statements aloud in 10 languages (English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, Mandarin Chinese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Portuguese, French, Tagalog, Korean).
3. **Optical Warrant Camera & OCR Scanner**: Real-time diagnostic pass analyzing document headers, form codes (Form I-200 vs Search Warrant), and signatory lines with colored bounding boxes and spoken verdict playback.
4. **Judicial vs. Administrative ICE Warrant Diagnostic Verifier**: 3-step inspection tool checking Court headers, Form I-200/I-205 numbers, and Judge vs ICE Officer signatures to prevent unlawful entry into homes.
5. **Interactive Encounter Defense Training Simulator**: Gamified scenario-based legal training module testing home door stops, passenger silence, SRO youth interrogations, and issuing Level 1 Constitutional Defender Certificates.
6. **Youth Miranda & SRO Interrogation Defense**: Specialized protections under *J.D.B. v. North Carolina* (minor age custody standard) and *In re Gault* with 1-click downloadable Youth Miranda Cards.
7. **50-State Sanctuary vs. 287(g) Detainer Policies Matrix**: Interactive policy explorer detailing state TRUST acts, ICE detainer limits, and high-risk local enforcement mandates.
8. **Interactive Family Safety Plan & Caregiver Affidavit Generator**: Interactive wizard for designating emergency child guardians and power of attorney with local device sanctuary privacy lock.
9. **6 Specialized Encounter Scenarios**: Comprehensive step-by-step survival protocols for Front Door stops, Juvenile & Youth Squad interrogations, Street confrontations, Traffic checkpoints, Workplace raids, and Sensitive Sanctuaries.
10. **Community Raid & Checkpoint Radar**: Live community-reported sightings with observer verification and alert dispatch.
11. **1-Tap Mobile Bottom Bar ICE Shield Launcher**: Dedicated bottom navigation button for instant 1-tap mobile access during an active encounter.
12. **24/7 Verified Immigration Legal Hotlines**: Direct toll-free access to United We Dream, ACLU Immigrants' Rights, National Lawyers Guild, and the Florence Project.
