# JUSTICE PULSE ⚖️
### *The National Police Accountability Docket, Legal Facebook & Community Sanctuary Network*

**JUSTICE PULSE** is a community-first civic technology platform designed to bring absolute transparency to police misconduct in the United States, empower victims and their families with immediate pro bono legal defense and mutual aid, and provide an immutable evidence locker to prevent repeat offender officers ("Gypsy Cops") from escaping accountability.

---

## 🌟 Core Pillars & Key Features

### 1. 🏛️ The Public Square (The "Legal Facebook" Community Feed)
* **Social Dispatches & Eyewitness Feeds**: Facebook-style rich posting with verified incident tags, SHA-256 evidence previews, and FOIA document links.
* **Community Solidarity**: Interactive "Stand With Them" badges, comments, shares, and real-time reactions.
* **Persona Switcher**: Seamlessly preview the platform from the perspective of a Civil Rights Advocate, Civil Rights Litigator, or Community Eyewitness.

### 2. 📜 The National Docket (Verified Case Registry)
* **Documented Injustices**: In-depth case profiles (Sonya Massey, Tyre Nichols, Breonna Taylor, Elijah McClain, Tamir Rice, Marcus Delgado, etc.).
* **Chronological Timeline**: Step-by-step verified events from 911 dispatch calls to federal grand jury indictments.
* **Taxpayer Settlement Tracker**: Details municipal dollar amounts paid by public funds vs individual officer accountability.
* **One-Click Action**: Sign national petitions and auto-generate formal letters to District Attorneys.

### 3. 🛡️ Officer Index (Repeat Offender & Brady List Registry)
* **Gypsy Cop Tracking**: Multi-agency employment history tracking officers who jump from precinct to precinct following sustained misconduct.
* **Brady List Disclosure Flags**: Highlights officers flagged for perjury, dishonesty, or excessive force.
* **Complaints vs Sustained Findings**: Clear metrics on internal affairs discipline rates.

### 4. 🤝 Sanctuary Hub & Mutual Aid
* **Direct Family Relief (0% Platform Fee)**: 100% direct pass-through crowdfunding for victim memorial trusts and children's education funds.
* **24/7 Crisis & Legal Response**: Direct connections to emergency civil rights intake and trauma healing counselors.
* **Pro Bono Legal Network**: Connects low-income individuals with vetted civil rights law clinics across 50 states.

### 5. 📱 Know Your Rights & SOS Encounter Mode
* **Constitutional Defense Pocket Guide**: Word-for-word legal scripts for Traffic Stops, Recording Police (1st Amendment), and Home Search Warrants (4th Amendment).
* **Interactive SOS Live Encounter Mode**: Simulates off-device cloud video streaming, GPS geostamping, and emergency SMS alerts to loved ones and legal observers.
* **Interactive Knowledge Quiz**: Educational tools to test knowledge on search and seizure rights.

### 6. 📊 National Misconduct & Settlement Analytics
* **$3.28B+ Database**: State-by-state and city-by-city breakdown of taxpayer money spent on police misconduct settlements.
* **Interactive Taxpayer Misconduct Calculator**: Estimates how much of your local annual taxes subsidize police brutality settlements.

### 7. 🔒 Cryptographic Evidence Vault
* **Chain of Custody**: Cryptographic SHA-256 hash checksums ensuring digital evidence (bodycam, bystander footage, dispatch audio) is tamper-proof for court admissibility.

### 8. 💼 Investor & Stakeholder Pitch Deck
* **Investor Mode**: Interactive presentation covering the $4.8B Civic Tech TAM, SaaS revenue model for civil rights law firms, grant strategy, and traction metrics.

## 🚀 Quick Start Instructions

### Running the App:
1. Double-click `launch.bat` in this folder, or open a terminal here and run:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open your browser to `http://localhost:3000`.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **Frontend**: React 18, Vite
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Dark theme, glassmorphism, responsive UI)
* **Icons**: Lucide React
* **Effects**: Canvas Confetti for celebratory civic actions
* **Design Philosophy**: High-contrast, intuitive, emotionally resonant, and courtroom-ready.
