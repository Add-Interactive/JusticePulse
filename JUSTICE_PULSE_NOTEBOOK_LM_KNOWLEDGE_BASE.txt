2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & SYSTEMIC INDUSTRY NEED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The $3.4 Billion Annual Misconduct Taxpayer Crisis: Over $3.4B is paid annually by American 
  cities to settle excessive force claims ($12M Breonna Taylor, $27M George Floyd, $19M Elijah McClain).
• Aggressive Specialized Tactical Squads & ICE Encroachment: Vulnerable communities and youth face 
  aggressive street stops and door-to-door administrative sweeps. Citizens frequently succumb to 
  coercive entry due to confusing administrative papers (DHS Form I-200/I-205) with valid Judicial Search Warrants.
• Coercive Interrogation of Minors: School Resource Officers (SROs) subject minors to custodial 
  interrogations without parental presence, violating J.D.B. v. North Carolina (564 U.S. 261) and In re Gault.
• Siloed "Brady Lists" & The Wandering Officer Loophole: Brady lists remain siloed in county paper binders, 
  enabling officers terminated for perjury or brutality in one county to be rehired in another.
• Bodycam Redaction Bottlenecks & Video Alteration Risks: Multi-camera footage is delayed and 
  expensive to synchronize manually.
